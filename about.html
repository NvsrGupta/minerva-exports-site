ABOUT US

From quarry to port, we manage the full export process.

Minerva Exports sources, cuts, and ships natural granite and marble from Andhra Pradesh to buyers around the world.

We work directly with quarries to select consistent material, and we handle finishing, packaging, and export documentation so buyers receive stone that's ready to use on arrival.

Sourcing

Quarried directly in Andhra Pradesh, India.

Finishing

Cut, polished, and packaged to buyer specification.

Export

Shipped internationally with full export documentation.
